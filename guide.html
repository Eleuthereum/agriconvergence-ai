What’s in the guide

Six short chapters on how digital trails, records, and models show up in agriculture. Written for people who work around grain, farm-tech, and food supply records. It explains mechanisms. It does not tell you to buy, sell, or originate anything.

  1. Traceability and provenance

    What a lot identity, an event, an attester, and a lookup actually are — and why commingled grain breaks map-pin stories.

  2. Farm-asset records, not tickers

    How a transferable record differs from a listed coin, and why title still lives in contracts and warehouse books.

  3. Yield and input models

    Treat a model as a range with a date. What belongs in a tool, and what does not belong in a caption.

  4. Insurance and offtake scripts

    Parametric triggers, oracles, and the paperwork that remains after a script runs.

  5. Carbon and environmental rails

    Protocols, registries, retirement serials — and why a confident app is not a credit.

  6. Where commercial value shows up

    Buyers, auditors, lenders, and insurers. Friction someone already pays — not a new asset class.
