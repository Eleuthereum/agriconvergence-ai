A map of the meeting

Six short chapters on what happens when the physical lot, the model, and the shared record have to live in the same conversation. Written for people around grain, farm-tech, and food supply records. Mechanisms only. It does not tell you to buy, sell, or originate anything.

  1. Traceability — the lot

    Identity, events, attester, lookup. Why commingled grain breaks “farm on a map,” and why blockchain does not create the intake ticket.

  2. Records — not tickers

    A transferable pointer to a lot or a claim. Title still lives in contracts and warehouse books. A listed coin is out of scope.

  3. Models — ranges with dates

    Weather, soil, imagery, farm records in; a distribution out. AI as nowcast and exception list, not an oracle.

  4. Scripts on boring triggers

    Parametric insurance and offtake: rainfall, grade, delivery. The oracle problem, and the paperwork a script does not retire.

  5. Carbon rails

    Protocol, project, registry, retirement. Measurement is the bottleneck. A confident app is not a credit.

  6. Where the money already is

    Buyers, auditors, lenders, insurers — friction someone already pays. Not Telegram, not a farm-token listing.
