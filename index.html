What is converging

The field, the model, and the record are becoming one job.

Grain still moves as lots, bins, and barges. Software now reads the tickets and flags the exceptions. Shared records let parties who do not share a spreadsheet agree on the same event. That meeting is the story — not a coin, not a satellite poster.

Field

The lot still wins

Agriculture is physical: identity, commingle, grade, and who attested. If those are missing, no app can invent a trail after the pit.

Model

AI as a clerk, not an oracle

Capture, matching, exception lists, ranges with dates. A confident bushels-per-acre number with no spread is a poster.

Record

Ledgers as lookup

A shared event log is a tool for people who do not trust each other’s files. A public ticker is a different object.

Where they meet. Buyers want fewer surprises. Auditors want an export. Insurers want a trigger they can name. Lenders want a cleaner conversation. The $20 guide is a map of that convergence — mechanisms, not a listing.
